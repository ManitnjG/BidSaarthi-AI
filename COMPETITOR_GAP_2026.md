# BidSaarthi competitor gap review — September 2026

Public competitor pages reviewed: Tender247 and TenderTiger. Claims below are competitor-published and must not be copied as unverified BidSaarthi claims.

## Capabilities users now expect
- Natural-language/AI search plus advanced filters.
- Evidence-grounded tender summaries and eligibility / bid-no-bid assistance.
- Full tender documents, BOQ and corrigendum tracking where publicly accessible.
- Email/WhatsApp/mobile alerts and deadline monitoring.
- Bid workspace: tasks, approvals, document repository, EMD/BG/compliance tracking.
- Historical tender results, bidder/competitor and pricing intelligence.
- Excel/CSV/PDF exports and management reports.
- Team access, RBAC/MFA/audit logs for business plans.

## BidSaarthi differentiation to build
1. Evidence-first AI: every material eligibility claim links to an exact official excerpt/document.
2. Transparent UNKNOWN verdict whenever evidence is incomplete.
3. Exact official source links/reference numbers; never disguise a portal homepage as a tender notice.
4. Business DNA matching with explicit blockers and missing evidence.
5. Mobile-first Tamil + English workflow.
6. Source-health/coverage transparency instead of unsupported “all tenders” claims.

## Build priority
P0: document/corrigendum ingestion, typed AI extraction, Business DNA eligibility, source/link health.
P1: authenticated saved/profile/alerts, PostgreSQL, server entitlements and AI quotas.
P2: natural-language search, BOQ extraction, exports, email/WhatsApp alerts.
P3: historical awards/results, competitor/bidder analytics, team/RBAC/workflow.
P4: annexure assistance and document preparation with mandatory human verification.

Never automate submission to government portals by bypassing authentication, CAPTCHA or portal controls.
